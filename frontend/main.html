About Blockchain Land Registry

Secure • Transparent • Tamper-Proof Property Management System

📘 Project Overview

The Blockchain-Based Land Registry System provides a secure and transparent platform for managing land ownership records. Traditional systems often suffer from fraud, duplicate ownership, and lack of transparency.

By integrating Hyperledger Fabric with CouchDB, this system ensures all land records are immutable, traceable, and securely stored.
